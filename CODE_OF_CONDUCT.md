# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Contributors and maintainers are
expected to communicate constructively, accept technical disagreement, respect privacy, and keep
feedback focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats,
doxxing, publishing another person's private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation
against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, chat
spaces, and public events where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or
permanently ban participants when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be
proportionate, documented privately, and protect the reporter's confidentiality.

To report conduct privately, use a private contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub
profile. If none is available, open a public issue requesting private maintainer contact without
including sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
